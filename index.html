Bucher & Rogers Family Tree

Family history research for the Bucher and Rogers families

The Bucher and Rogers families of Franklin and Bedford Counties, Tennessee. All eight great-grandparents of Danny and Brenda Bucher's children are named; the Bucher side reaches 1500s Netherlands and 1600s England and Virginia, with a documented Revolutionary War colonel, and the Rogers side stops in 1800s Tennessee for now. Built from 23andMe, the family's MyHeritage tree (Hosler Family Site), a 2018 Ancestry.com family tree, WikiTree, family Bibles, wills, obituaries and grave records, with each claim checked.

Confidence ratings and source links are kept for each claim. Research status as of Oct 28, 2026.

Civil War relatives

For family members interested in joining the United Daughters of the Confederacy (UDC) or Sons of Confederate Veterans (SCV), as of Oct 28, 2026; relations are counted from Jeremiah and his siblings

The UDC accepts direct descendants of a Confederate soldier, or of his brother. His service has to be honorable, with no desertion, and he can't have taken the Union oath of allegiance before April 9, 1865. A tombstone isn't accepted as proof. A National Archives service record, a state pension file, or an official published roster is.
